## Known reconciling items

From `config/reconciling-items.csv`:

- **amortization** (PA higher): StatCan CCOFOG excludes consumption of fixed capital (table footnote 2). Public accounts expense includes amortization of tangible capital assets.
- **capital_acquisitions** (none on expense): StatCan also excludes acquisition of non-financial assets. Public accounts expense excludes capital purchases too (they are capitalised), so there is no expense-basis gap; it matters only if you compare against spending including capital.
- **reporting_perimeter** (either): Provincial summary statements consolidate hospitals, school boards and (in most provinces) colleges, and usually not universities. StatCan's provincial_territorial component is government proper and counts its grants to those bodies as its own expense. Spending those bodies fund from their own revenue (fees, donations, federal research grants) is in the public accounts but not in provincial_territorial.
- **no_elimination** (StatCan higher if components summed): StatCan does not eliminate transfers between components (footnote 3). Adding the provincial_territorial component to the hospital/school board/college components double-counts the provincial grants, so the sum is an upper bound, not a comparator.
- **interest_classification** (none at division level): StatCan puts all interest in 01 General public services (footnote 6). Map public-accounts interest to 01.7 to match.
- **functional_classification** (reallocates between divisions): Ministry-to-COFOG mapping is approximate: e.g. Ontario child care sits in Education (COFOG 10.4), long-term care in Health (COFOG may use 10.2), central employee benefits in 01. These move amounts between divisions without changing the total.
- **accounting_basis** (either): Public accounts follow Canadian public sector accounting standards; StatCan converts to Government Finance Statistics. Pension expense, provisions and some one-time items are measured differently.
- **vintage** (either): StatCan's latest year is marked preliminary (status p) and is revised. Public accounts comparatives are restated (e.g. Ontario Note 17).
